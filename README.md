# 📊 Supply Chain Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor
supply chain performance across sales, profit, customers, products,
shipping, and orders.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Supply Chain Performance Dashboard
built with Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard transforms business data into interactive visual insights
that help understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior,
product performance, shipping efficiency, and order activity.

The dashboard is designed for both executive-level overview and
detailed business analysis.

---

## 💼 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance
- Track sales and profit trends over time
- Analyze performance by product category
- Identify top-performing products
- Identify top-performing customers
- Analyze customer segments
- Evaluate shipping performance
- Analyze order volume and quantity
- Compare regional performance
- Monitor key business KPIs
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 🏠 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the overall business performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders

**Main Analysis:**
- Sales Trend by Month
- Sales by Category
- Total Profit by Month
- Top 5 Products
- Key Business Insights

---

### 💰 2. Sales Analysis

Provides detailed analysis of sales performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Sales YoY %
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

**Main Analysis:**
- Sales Trend by Month
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Order Region
- Top 5 Products

---

### 📈 3. Profit Analysis

Focuses on profitability and profit performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Average Profit per Order
- Profit YoY %

**Main Analysis:**
- Profit Trend by Month
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Order Region
- Top 5 Products by Profit

---

### 👥 4. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer behavior and performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Customers
- Profit per Customer
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

**Main Analysis:**
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 5 Customers by Sales
- Sales Trend by Month
- Top 5 Customers by Profit

---

### 📦 5. Product Analysis

Analyzes product and category performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Products
- Total Sales
- Average Product Price
- Total Profit

**Main Analysis:**

- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Sales Trend by Month
- Top 5 Products by Profit
